Game Mechanics

Understanding the simulation

🌊 World Trends

Every few months, trends shift boosting certain genres. Ride the wave for 1.5x hype multipliers. Check the trends panel to know what's hot!

📉 Hype Decay

Your hype naturally decays over time. Keep releasing music or performing gigs to maintain visibility. High hype = better gig payouts.

🔥 Burnout System

Constant production leads to burnout. High burnout reduces track quality. Rest by taking breaks between production cycles.

💰 Royalties & Revenue

Self-releases earn $0.004/stream. Label releases earn $0.008/stream (split per label agreement). Build fans for exponential growth.

🎯 Track Quality Formula

Track quality is calculated from:

  • Gear bonuses — Equipment upgrades directly improve stats
  • Skill levels — Skills provide percentage multipliers
  • Secondary genres — Adding a secondary genre boosts originality
  • Label support — Signed artists get additional quality boosts

Tips for Success

Strategic advice for all game phases

🚀 Early Game (Months 1-6)

1

Build Initial Fanbase

Release frequently, even if quality is modest. Consistent output builds organic following.

2

Save for Headphones

Studio headphones ($190) give immediate mixing quality boost. Best early investment.

3

Experiment with Genres

Try adding secondary genres for the +25% originality bonus. Stand out from the crowd.

🌟 Mid Game (Months 6-24)

4

Target Matching Labels

Sign with labels that match your genre. Early labels like Subterranean Clicks are easier to get.

5

Gig Before Major Releases

Build hype through performances before dropping important tracks for maximum impact.

6

Invest in Skills

Skill points provide permanent bonuses. Prioritize skills matching your genre focus.

👑 Late Game (Month 24+)

7

Target Festival Venues

Maximum payouts at festival-tier venues. London, Detroit, and LA festivals pay $14k-$140k.

8

Manage Burnout

Take strategic rest periods between intense production. Quality > Quantity at this stage.

9

Ride Trends

When a trend boosts your genre, release heavily to maximize the 1.5x hype multiplier.
